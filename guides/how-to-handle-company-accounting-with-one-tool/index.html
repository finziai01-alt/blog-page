How to handle your company accounting with one tool.

The short answer: route every document to one place, let the reading happen automatically, approve entries as they arrive, and hand your accountant books that are already current. Here is the full workflow.

Why accounting feels hard right now

It's rarely the accounting itself. It's the paper. Bills arrive as phone photos, courier slips, and email attachments. Someone has to read each one and type it in. Bank statements arrive monthly and someone has to tick off payments, including the client who paid in parts. By month-end, the work has piled into a marathon, and your accountant starts from reconstruction instead of review.

The fix isn't another form or another register. It's changing where the effort goes.

The one-tool workflow, step by step

  1. Use one inbox for every document. Every bill (photo, scan or PDF) and every bank statement goes to a single place. Nothing lives in WhatsApp threads or email folders where it can be forgotten.
  2. Let the reading happen automatically. The tool reads vendor, invoice number, date, sub-total, GST breakup and total from each bill, and finds every transaction in each statement. Your job becomes checking, not typing.
  3. Approve as documents arrive. Review the extracted fields beside the original document and approve them. Seconds per bill beats a month-end marathon every time.
  4. Let reminders handle due dates. Scheduled reminders take memory out of the process.
  5. Give your accountant current books. When records are already approved and matched, your accountant reviews and advises, instead of reconstructing history.

What the “one tool” must actually do

  • Read bills from photos, PDFs and scans, with GST exactly as printed
  • Match bank statements automatically, with instalments shown honestly as Partial Payments
  • Gate every entry behind your approval: nothing saves without you
  • Send due-date reminders
  • Answer report questions in plain English
  • Run in a browser. No installation, no training

Common questions

One tool can handle the document work: reading bills, matching statements, preparing approved entries and reminders. Your accountant's judgment, review and filings stay with your accountant; the tool makes their work faster.

How much time does approve-as-you-go take?

Seconds per bill. Approve a batch while your morning coffee is brewing instead of facing a month-end marathon.

Automatic reading of bills and statements, an approval gate before anything saves, honest tracking of part-payments, reminders for due dates, plain-English reports, and access from any browser.
