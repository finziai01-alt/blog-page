How to stop re-typing bills into your accounting software.

Every bill that arrives as a photo or PDF eventually becomes keystrokes — someone reads it, someone types it, someone checks it twice. Here is the workflow that removes the typing while keeping the checking.

Why the typing keeps coming back

Scanning a bill is not the problem. The problem is what happens next: the bill still has to become an entry. So someone opens the accounting software and types vendor, invoice number, date, amounts and GST — line by line, for every bill, every month. Software that "imports" only moves the problem when the source is already structured; most Indian bills arrive as phone photos, courier slips and WhatsApp PDFs, not structured data.

The fix is to change what a bill is when it arrives: not a piece of paper to be transcribed, but a document to be read and approved.

The four-step workflow

  1. Collect bills as they are. Photograph paper bills, forward emailed PDFs, drag in scans. One inbox, no conversion step, no printing.
  2. Read every field automatically. Vendor, invoice number, date, sub-total, GST breakup and total — read from the document itself. GST is taken exactly as printed. If a total isn't printed on the bill, the calculated figure is flagged for you to verify, never silently guessed.
  3. Approve beside the original. Every extracted field appears next to the original document, so checking takes seconds per bill. Nothing enters your books until you approve it — the human stays the control, not the bottleneck.
  4. Let bank matching catch the payments. When the bank statement arrives, payments match against the approved entries automatically. A client who paid in instalments is stacked and shown as a partial payment, not wrongly marked fully paid.

What to demand from any "bill reading" tool

  • Fields read from photos, PDFs and scans — not just neat digital invoices
  • GST breakup read as printed, with calculated totals clearly flagged
  • Every field shown beside the original before anything saves
  • No entry, no payment action, no change without your approval
  • Works in a browser on phone, tablet or laptop — nothing to install

Before and after, honestly

Before: a pile of photos and PDFs; an hour (or several) of typing; a second pass to catch typos; the bill still needs checking against the entry anyway.

After: the same pile of photos and PDFs; fields already read and shown beside the original; your time spent approving, not typing. The checking you were going to do anyway is now the entire job.

Common questions

Can bill reading replace typing completely?

It replaces the keystrokes, not the judgement. The tool reads the fields; you approve them. Unusual formats or missing totals are flagged for you to verify rather than guessed at.

What about handwritten or faded bills?

Clear photos of printed bills read reliably. Handwritten or heavily faded documents are where you should check more carefully — treat the output as a draft and verify against the original, which is exactly what the approval step is for.

Will my accountant get the data in their software?

Finzi produces approved ledger entries and exportable files your accountant can work from. The goal is that your accountant reviews and advises instead of re-keying history.
