Common questions

Do I need to know accounting?

No. You see what Finzi read beside your original bill and approve it, field by field. Your accountant works with the results.

What if a bill has no total printed on it?

Finzi calculates the total and clearly asks you to verify it before anything is saved.

How are instalments handled?

Honestly. A client paying in parts is stacked and shown as a Partial Payment; the books never wrongly say “fully paid”.
