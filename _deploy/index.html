How Finzi works, in under a minute

Three moves. No forms, no training, no software to install.

  1. 1

    Drop your bill

    A photograph, a scanned copy, an emailed PDF. Drag it in. Bank statements go the same way, as files or CSV.

  2. 2

    Finzi reads every field

    Vendor, invoice number, date, sub-total, GST breakup and total, read in seconds. If a total isn't printed on the bill, Finzi calculates it and clearly asks you to verify.

  3. 3

    You approve, then it saves

    Every extracted field is shown beside your original document. You check it, you approve it, and only then does it enter your books.

Questions people ask before they start

What is Finzi AI?

Finzi AI is an AI accounting assistant for Indian accountants and small businesses. It reads bills (photos, PDFs, scans) and bank statements. Vendor, invoice number, date, sub-total, GST breakup and total are shown for approval. Nothing enters the books until a person approves it.

Who is Finzi AI for?

Accounting practices and chartered accountants in India use it for their clients' books. Small and mid-sized businesses use it to keep their own books current without data entry.

Does Finzi AI work with GST bills?

Yes. GST is read exactly as printed. If a total isn't printed on the bill, Finzi calculates it and clearly asks you to verify before anything is saved.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Finzi runs in a browser on your phone, tablet or laptop. There are no forms to fill in: drag-and-drop is the entire interaction.

Is my data safe?

Documents are encrypted in transit, records live in a private sign-in-protected workspace, and every entry passes an approval gate. Finzi never sells or shares your data.
