Finzi AI vs Tally: different tools, different jobs.

Tally is established accounting software for running the books themselves. Finzi AI is the document layer in front of them: reading bills and statements and collecting approvals so nobody re-keys. Here is an honest, category-level look at where each fits.

The honest answer

Most of the pain around accounting in small businesses isn't the ledger. It's everything before it. Bills sitting unread, payments half-matched, approvals chased by phone. Finzi AI is built exactly for that layer. Tally's category solves a different problem: keeping the complete books. If your team already runs on it, Finzi doesn't ask you to leave. It removes the typing and the chasing in front of it.

Questions people ask

Is Finzi AI a replacement for Tally?

No. Finzi prepares clean, approved records from your bills and statements. Tally is established accounting software for running the books themselves. Most teams let Finzi remove the reading and re-keying, then work with the records where they already operate.

Can I use them together?

Yes. Finzi does the document work (reading bills, matching statements, collecting approvals), so the records are ready to hand over to your existing process.

Which should I choose?

If your pain is re-keying bills and chasing part-payments, Finzi addresses that directly. If you need a complete accounting program to run GST, inventory and compliance, that is Tally's category, and Finzi can still sit in front of it.
