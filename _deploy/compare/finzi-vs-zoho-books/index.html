Finzi AI vs Zoho Books: the document layer or the whole suite.

Zoho Books is a cloud accounting suite for running the business's books. Finzi AI is what happens before the books: bills read, statements matched, entries approved. An honest look at where each belongs.

The honest answer

Suites like Zoho Books are built to hold the whole accounting picture: invoicing, expenses, reports, compliance workflows. That's valuable, and it's not what Finzi does. Finzi solves the step everyone skips past: getting documents read and approved without a human typing session. If your bills pile up before they ever reach your accounting software, Finzi is the missing layer, not a competitor.

Questions people ask

Is Finzi AI a replacement for Zoho Books?

No. Zoho Books is a cloud accounting suite for running the business's books. Finzi is the document layer: it reads bills and bank statements and prepares entries a person approves, so the typing disappears.

Can they work together?

Yes. Finzi prepares clean, approved records from documents; your team keeps working in the accounting suite it already uses.

Which should I choose?

If the problem is documents (bills unread, statements unmatched, approvals chased), Finzi addresses it directly. If you need a full cloud accounting platform, that is Zoho Books' category, and Finzi can still sit in front of it.
