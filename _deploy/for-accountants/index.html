Where the practice hours go, and where they go instead

Re-keying disappears

When a client emails a stack of bills, nobody types them in. Finzi reads the vendor, invoice number, date, GST breakup and total, and lays every field out for approval in seconds.

Instalments stay honest

Bank statements are matched automatically, and a client paying in parts is shown as a Partial Payment, never wrongly marked “fully paid”. No more double-marking, no more questions at month-end.

Books current between visits

Clients approve entries as documents arrive, so records are tidy before you sit down: fewer chases, faster month-end, better advice.

Reports ready for you

FAQs from practices

How does Finzi AI help a CA firm?

It removes re-keying. Clients' bills and bank statements are read field by field and prepared as clean entries. Instalment payments are tracked honestly as Partial Payments. Your practice reviews and advises instead of typing.

Who approves the entries?

A person — the client or your team — sees every extracted field beside the original document and approves it. Nothing enters the books without that approval.

Does Finzi AI replace my accounting software?

No. Finzi prepares clean, current records and matches payments. Your practice keeps its own review, filing and advisory process; the records are ready to hand over.

Is each client's data separate?

Yes. Each client works in a private, sign-in-protected workspace, and documents are encrypted in transit. Finzi never sells or shares data.
