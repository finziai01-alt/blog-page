What approving looks like

  1. See the fields: vendor, invoice number, date, sub-total, GST breakup and total, laid out beside the original document.
  2. Check against the source: your eye on the paper is the final word. Anything unclear gets caught here, not at month-end.
  3. Approve, then it saves: only what you approve enters the ledger. No silent writes, no surprises in the books.

Why we built it this way

Fully automatic bookkeeping sounds convenient until an error reaches your filings. Finzi keeps the speed of AI reading and leaves judgment where it belongs: with the person accountable for the numbers. It's the difference between a tool that guesses and a tool you can trust with your business.

For practices: the approval gate means a client's mistakes never silently become your problem. What you review is what was actually approved.

FAQs about approval-first

Who approves the entries?

A person you choose — the business owner or the practice team. The gate applies to every entry, every payment match and every change.

Doesn't approval-first slow things down?

Approving a set of checked fields takes seconds, far less than typing them. The slow part was never the checking; it was the re-keying.

Why not just automate everything?

Because books must be true. Approval-first keeps the speed of AI reading while leaving judgment with the person accountable for the numbers.
