What Finzi reads from each bill

Every bill is read field by field, then shown beside the original document:

  • Vendor: the supplier's name as printed
  • Invoice number: exactly as printed, no re-typing
  • Date: the bill date
  • Sub-total: before tax
  • GST breakup: read exactly as printed on the bill
  • Total: and if a total isn't printed, Finzi calculates it and clearly asks you to verify

Why approval comes before saving

Reading is automatic; saving is not. Every extracted field sits beside your original bill, and nothing enters the books until you approve it. That is the whole design: fast reading, human judgment, honest books.

Try it on your messiest bill. A crumpled photo with a faint print is a fair test: Finzi shows you what it read, and you decide what saves.

FAQs about bill reading

Which bills can Finzi AI read?

Photographs, scanned copies and emailed PDFs. There are no forms to fill in: you drop the bill in and Finzi lays out every field it read.

How is GST handled?

GST is read exactly as printed. If a total isn't printed, Finzi calculates it and clearly asks you to verify before anything is saved.

What if something was read incorrectly?

You'll catch it before anything saves: every field is shown beside the original document, and approval is the gate. Only what you approve enters the books.
