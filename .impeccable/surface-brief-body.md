# Finzi AI marketing site

Mode: **Persuade**. Audience: Indian accounting practices (primary) and their SMB clients (secondary), arriving from search, AI answers, or a shared link. Visitor job: within one screen, believe that Finzi AI reads bills and statements with a person approving every entry — then book a demo or start free.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The first viewport shows the mechanism itself — a bill becoming checked fields beside a ledger row that waits for a human tick — refusing the generic SaaS hero of headline plus stock dashboard.
OWN-WORLD: Locked brand blues (#1E40AF / #3B82F6 / #93C5FD) on white with slate ink (#1E293B); soft 16px-radius panels, 1px #E2E8F0 borders, layered soft shadows; geometric-sans stack (Segoe UI / Inter per brochure); authored 1.5px-stroke SVG icons; the demo panel is real HTML/CSS (agent-readable, zero rasters).
STORY: Visitor understands "documents in, verified entries out, with me approving" and books a demo; accountants see their practice's pain (re-keying, part-payments) answered directly.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left — headline "Bills read. Payments matched. Books honest.", a plain support line, primary "Book a demo" and secondary "See how it works". Right — demo panel: bill card, extracted-fields card with a sequential check-in, and a "Saved to ledger — approved by you" row. Mobile stacks with the panel following the headline.
FORM: code-led (recorded in .impeccable/config.json); one authored motion moment — the fields check-in sequence, reduced-motion safe.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
