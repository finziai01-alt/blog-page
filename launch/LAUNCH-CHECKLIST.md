# Finzi AI — 7-day launch checklist (₹0 budget)

Tick items off as you go. Everything here is free. Nothing here needs a card.

## Day 1 — Live + connected
- [ ] Deploy the site (follow `launch\DEPLOY.md`)
- [ ] Google Search Console: property added + sitemap submitted + homepage indexing requested
- [ ] Bing Webmaster Tools: site added (import from GSC) + sitemap submitted
- [ ] Record the AI-visibility baseline (`launch\AI-VISIBILITY-BASELINE.md`) — do this BEFORE telling anyone about the site

## Day 2 — Foundation check
- [ ] Re-check the live site on phone + desktop; fix anything broken
- [ ] Share the link on your WhatsApp status / LinkedIn / X once (first human visitors)
- [ ] Update the brochure + cold-call playbook footers with the new URL, re-generate PDFs

## Day 3 — Directories (free listings that AI assistants read)
Submit the same short description everywhere (copy it from `llms.txt`, first paragraph):
- [ ] Product Hunt — create the product page now, launch later when you want the spike: https://www.producthunt.com
- [ ] SaaSHub: https://www.saashub.com/submit
- [ ] AlternativeTo: https://alternativeto.net/manage/add/ (add as alternative to Tally / Zoho Books)
- [ ] SoftwareSuggest (India): https://www.softwaresuggest.com/vendors
- [ ] GetApp: https://www.getapp.com/join-us
- [ ] Capterra: same vendor signup as GetApp
- [ ] G2: https://www.g2.com/products/new
- [ ] Crunchbase (company page — free): https://www.crunchbase.com

## Day 4 — YouTube (your videos are already made)
- [ ] Create the Finzi AI YouTube channel (or use existing)
- [ ] Upload `finzi-hero-50s.mp4` (16:9) — title: "Finzi AI — Bills read. Payments matched. Books honest. (50s demo)"
- [ ] Upload `finzi-vertical-15s-9x16.mp4` as a **Short** — title: "Stop typing bills into your books | Finzi AI"
- [ ] Paste the site description + link into both descriptions; add the full text of the 50s video's narration as the description/transcript text (AI systems read it)
- [ ] Pin a comment: "Try it free — your first bills are on us. → <your site link>"

## Day 5 — Communities (be genuinely helpful, never spam)
- [ ] Reddit: answer 2–3 real questions on r/IndiaTax, r/india, r/smallbusiness_india where someone asks about bookkeeping pain. Help first; mention Finzi only if truly relevant. One good answer beats ten links.
- [ ] Quora: search "accounting app India", "Tally alternative", "how to manage company accounts" — answer 2 questions with real substance
- [ ] LinkedIn: post the 15s video with a short story about re-keying pain (2–3 sentences, no hype). Ask your CA contacts to share.

## Day 6 — Outreach (the fastest AI-citation lever)
- [ ] Find 15–20 articles titled "best accounting apps India" / "best AI tools for accountants" (Google them; note the author's email or Twitter)
- [ ] Send each a short, personal email: who you are, what Finzi does in one line, ask to be included in their next update. Template tone: honest, no "revolutionary".
- [ ] Free PR pitch to Indian startup media: YourStory (editorial@yourstory.com), Inc42 — one paragraph + the demo video + site link
- [ ] Ask 3 friendly CAs/business owners who have seen the demo to leave a review on your G2/SaaSHub/Product Hunt page (never write reviews yourself — it's detectable and it backfires)

## Day 7 — Measure + decide next week
- [ ] Search Console: check how many pages are indexed (Coverage/Pages report)
- [ ] Re-run the AI-visibility battery (same prompts as baseline) — record results
- [ ] Compare with baseline; note which competitors appear where Finzi should
- [ ] Pick next week's 3 priorities from what you saw (usually: more listicle inclusions, fresh Q&A content, directory reviews)

---

**Ground rules (why the checklist looks like this):** no paid ads, no bought links, no fake
reviews, no spam. At ₹0 the only real currency is being genuinely useful in the places
search engines and AI assistants already read. That compounds — quietly, then suddenly.
