# Finzi AI — AI visibility baseline (run on Day 1, repeat weekly)

Purpose: record what AI assistants recommend today, **before** the site and listings spread.
One run is an anecdote; the trend across weeks is the measurement.

## How to run

- Platforms (free tiers are fine): **ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude**.
- Ask each prompt **3 times per platform** (answers vary). Log how many of 3 runs mention Finzi.
- Paste the exact prompt, no extra context. New conversation each time.
- Record: date, platform, prompt number, Finzi mentioned? (0/3–3/3), which competitors were named, any sources cited.

## The 20 prompts

**Product-aware (brand) — these should come good first:**
1. What is Finzi AI?
2. Finzi AI accounting assistant — is it good?
3. Finzi AI vs Tally
4. Finzi AI pricing

**Solution-aware (category) — the main battlefield:**
5. Best AI accounting app for small businesses in India
6. Best AI tool for chartered accountants in India
7. AI software that reads bills and GST invoices for accountants
8. App that matches bank statements and tracks partial payments
9. Best app to make accounting easy for a small company in India
10. CA AI tools — what should an Indian CA firm use in 2026?
11. AI bookkeeping software with human approval before saving
12. Which accounting app should a small business in India use to stop manual data entry?

**Problem-aware (long-tail) — where Finzi's guide should surface:**
13. How to handle my company accounting easily with one tool
14. How do I stop re-typing bills into my accounting software?
15. How to track a client who pays in instalments without marking them fully paid
16. Best way to read GST breakup from bill photos automatically

**Comparison / shopping — where listicles and directories matter:**
17. Tally alternative for small business India with AI
18. Zoho Books alternative that reads bills automatically
19. Best bookkeeping app for CA firms in India under budget
20. Accounting automation tools for Indian accountants — compare options

## Weekly cadence

- **Week 1:** full 20-prompt battery (baseline).
- **Weeks 2–8:** full battery once a week, same day/time.
- **After week 8:** monthly, plus whenever a listing/listicle goes live.

## What to do with the results

- Finzi named in 0/3 everywhere = normal on day 1. Re-run after the site is indexed and 10+ listings are live.
- Competitor named where Finzi isn't = note **where the assistant says it learned that** (it often cites sources) — those cited pages are your outreach targets.
- Finzi described wrongly = fix the source page (usually it's copying an old description; make `llms.txt` + the homepage the clearest version).
