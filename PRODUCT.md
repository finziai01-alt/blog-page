# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS, hosted on Cloudflare Pages (user's choice; free tier, drag-and-drop deploy). No framework, no build step.

## Users

Primary: accountants and accounting practices in India (chartered accountants and their teams) handling client bookkeeping — bills, GST documents, bank statements — who lose hours to re-keying and chasing approvals.
Secondary: small and mid-sized business clients of those practices who want their everyday books current without data entry.

## Product Purpose

Finzi AI reads bills (photos, PDFs, scans) and bank statements, extracts every field, and proposes clean ledger entries that a human approves before anything is saved. It exists so books are current, honest, and ready for the accountant without re-typing and without blind automation. Success means practices and their clients stop re-keying and month-end stops being a chase.

## Positioning

Approval-first AI bookkeeping: every extracted field is shown beside the original document, and nothing enters the books until a person approves it. Instalment payments are tracked honestly as Partial Payments, never wrongly marked fully paid.

## Operating Context

- Documents arrive as phone photos, scans, or emailed PDFs; bank statements as files or CSV — drag-and-drop is the entire interaction.
- Review happens in a browser on phone, tablet, or laptop; nothing to install.
- Work flows from client to practice: the accountant sees tidy, current records; clients get email reminders for due dates.
- Sales currently happen live (a cold-call playbook exists; goal is a 15-minute demo); there is no public app link yet — site CTAs invite a demo.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed: bill reading (vendor, invoice number, date, sub-total, GST breakup, total), totals calculated and clearly flagged for verification when not printed; bank statement matching; partial-payment tracking; approval gate on every entry; plain-English report questions; email reminders; records ready to hand to the accountant; browser-only, no install. Security: encrypted in transit, private sign-in workspace, data never sold or shared.
Undecided (must not be invented): exact pricing — the site may say only "Try it free — your first bills are on us."; public app URL; contact email/WhatsApp for demo requests.

## Product Principles

1. Nothing saves without a human — approval-first is both the trust story and the product.
2. Honest books beat fast books — partial payments shown honestly; calculated amounts flagged for verification.
3. The accountant stays the expert — Finzi prepares clean material; the professional reviews and advises.
4. Drag-and-drop is the entire skill — no forms, no training.
5. Every claim must be true today — no invented numbers, customers, or ratings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience reads English and Hindi; plain-language copy; comfortably readable at phone scale. No formal accessibility standard committed yet.
