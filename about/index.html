Why Finzi exists.

Finzi AI is an AI accounting assistant for Indian accountants and their clients. It reads the paperwork (bills, statements) and asks before it writes. That is the whole idea.

The belief behind it

Bookkeeping shouldn't be a typing job, and it shouldn't be a blind guess either. The hours people lose every month go into reading paper and retyping it, not into judgement. Finzi gives the reading to AI and keeps the judgement with the person: every field is shown beside the original document, every entry waits for approval.

Who builds it

Finzi AI is made by FARATECH TECHNOLOGIES, building accounting tools for the realities of Indian businesses and practices: GST bills, instalment payments, phone photos of paper and browser-first work.

What we won't do

  • Save anything without a person approving it
  • Mark a part-payment as “fully paid”
  • Sell or share your data
  • Publish numbers, customers or ratings we can't show you
