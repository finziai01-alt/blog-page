How matching works

  1. Drop in your statement: a PDF, an exported file, or CSV from your bank.
  2. Finzi finds every transaction: credit, debit, reference numbers, dates.
  3. Exact payments are matched: a bill paid in full is marked paid instantly.
  4. Instalments stack honestly: part-payments accumulate against the bill and it is shown as a Partial Payment, never wrongly marked “fully paid”.

Why honest beats “automatic”

Most tools optimise for less work. Finzi optimises for books you can trust. A client who has paid half a bill is a fact your books should show, not an inconvenience to smooth over. Every match passes the same approval gate as every bill: you see it, you approve it, then it saves.

The question Finzi answers every month: “How do I know nothing got marked paid twice?” Partial Payments keep a running, visible record of what actually arrived.

FAQs about matching

What statement formats can I use?

Bank statement files and CSV exports, the way your bank already gives them to you. Drag-and-drop is the whole process.

What exactly is a Partial Payment?

When a client pays in instalments, each payment stacks against the bill. The bill shows as a Partial Payment until it is actually paid in full, never earlier.

Does anything get matched and saved without me?

Matching is automatic; saving is not. Every match goes through the same approval gate as bills: nothing enters the books without a person approving it.
